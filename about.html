About North American Logistics

Dedicated International Transportation Services Since 1992

Our Story

Established in 1992, North American Logistics is a third-generation family-owned international transportation company providing dedicated, personalized freight forwarding services.


For over three decades, we have helped businesses move international cargo from locations around the world into the United States. Our commitment is built on strong relationships, dependable communication, and customized transportation solutions designed around each customer's unique needs.


As a family-owned company, we understand that every shipment represents an important commitment. Our experienced team works closely with each customer to provide professional service from origin to final delivery.

Our Commitment

At North American Logistics, our goal is simple: provide reliable, efficient, and personalized transportation services while maintaining the highest level of customer care. Whether managing ocean freight, air cargo, or domestic transportation, we focus on delivering solutions that help our customers succeed.
